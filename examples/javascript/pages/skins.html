Skins Example

The player comes with a dark skin (the default) and a light skin. Both players below load the same playlist with every feature turned on, so you can see how each skin looks across all of them:

  • Shoppable video (first item): product panel, hotspots and the post-play "Shop the Video" screen.
  • Chapters and auto-generated subtitles (second item): chapter markers, chapter name in the control bar, captions and the caption settings.
  • Recommendations (third item): the recommendations grid when the video ends (after you cancel the auto-advance countdown).
  • Playlist: the playlist panel, the "Up next" card in the last seconds, and the "Up next in N" countdown when a video ends.
  • Also: logo button, seek thumbnails, playback speeds and the right-click (Shift+F10) menu.

To use the light skin, add the video-js-skin-light class to the video element. The playlist panel has its own theme: add theme-dark to the playlist container for the dark version (done for the first player below).
